Hypixel documents a **separate** `GET /v2/skyblock/garden?profile=<profile-id>` endpoint. Its example exposes `garden_experience`, `resources_collected`, `crop_upgrade_levels`, `unlocked_plots_ids`, `commission_data`, `active_commissions`, and `composter_data`. A 404 means no Garden result from that endpoint, not Garden level zero. The app currently retains member `garden_player_data` and `jacobs_contest` as opaque values under `otherProgression`; it does not call the Garden endpoint. The research inspection uses both sources and records the endpoint status without serializing arbitrary raw objects.

The current Garden guide says crop milestones count Garden harvesting and separately grant Garden XP and Farming XP; collections can progress under different conditions. It lists 46 tiers per crop and separate visitor milestones. The December 2025 update reworked crop and visitor milestones and introduced new crops, so older threshold tables cannot be imported without an explicit current-source audit. Garden I starts on first Garden visit; no level-zero assumption should be applied to a missing Garden response.

The cited timer formula combines the unique base crop bonus (+2.5% each, up to 12), Crop Growth (+0.25% per point), Greenhouse Speed Attribute (+0.1% per point), and Growth Speed upgrade (+5% per level to VIII, +50% at IX) in its denominator. ## Mutation chance, unusual behaviors and slot acquisition (2026-09-29)

The [mutation table's staff-sourced reference](https://hypixelskyblock.minecraft.wiki/w/Mutations#References) lists weights for all 40 named mutations. Most common mutations have weight 30, most other ordinary recipes 25, four late recipes 20, Lonelily 6, Godseed 5, and special-trigger Shellfruit/Jerryflower 0. **A weight is not an actual player spawn chance.** The listed single-recipe chance assumes no competing mutation can occupy the same empty area, and surrounding *crop* support also changes it. A staff clarification explicitly gives Ashwreath 15% rather than its weight's nominal 30%, because Fire is not a crop and its two Nether Wart neighbors support half the roll; Bioanalysis Artifact changes that example to 17.25%. The API does not reveal the planted layout, competing eligible patterns, artifact activation or slot state. Consequently this pass exposes only reference weights, marks actual chance unreported, and does not derive spawn time, expected vine yield or profitability.

Specific cultivation and harvest rules alter whether a recipe can be used: [Snoozling](https://hypixelskyblock.minecraft.wiki/w/Snoozling) sleeps at stages 5/10/15 until woken; [Blastberry](https://hypixelskyblock.minecraft.wiki/w/Blastberry) explodes on harvest or decay, destroying neighboring crops except Blastberries and enabling Shellfruit from Turtlellini; [Magic Jellybean](https://hypixelskyblock.minecraft.wiki/w/Magic_Jellybean) becomes harvestable at stage 12 and scales drops every 12 stages to 10×; [PlantBoy Advance](https://hypixelskyblock.minecraft.wiki/w/PlantBoy_Advance) requires winning Snake or loses three stages; [Startlevine](https://hypixelskyblock.minecraft.wiki/w/Startlevine) requires Invisibility at harvest; [Stoplight Petal](https://hypixelskyblock.minecraft.wiki/w/Stoplight_Petal) is destroyed by a red-phase punch; [Chorus Fruit](https://hypixelskyblock.minecraft.wiki/w/Chorus_Fruit) teleports during growth; [Glasscorn](https://hypixelskyblock.minecraft.wiki/w/Glasscorn) must be taken at stage 7 or 8 or resets to 1; and [Timestalk](https://hypixelskyblock.minecraft.wiki/w/Timestalk) produces a clone that must be defeated to obtain the mutation. The mutation overview still says Glasscorn has 9 stages, while its current item page says **8** and records a reduction from 9; the catalog now follows the item page. Whether forced Chorus Fruit relocation destroys other crops is explicitly marked unconfirmed by that source and is not asserted as fact here. Behavior notes are reference rules; current live crop stages remain invisible.

The [Dead Plant](https://hypixelskyblock.minecraft.wiki/w/Dead_Plant) page clarifies the Witherbloom input: plants become Dead Plants at -100 water, and most mature mutations can decay into them after their individual timer, sometimes despite hydration. This is an acquisition route, but neither a Garden level gate nor an observed inventory quantity; the four-block Witherbloom layout remains unverified. The current [Ethereal Vine item page](https://hypixelskyblock.minecraft.wiki/w/Ethereal_Vine) says one Vine frees an adjacent locked planting slot; this is a spatial gate, so total needed depends on actual locked slots. Mutation harvest vine chances differ by rarity: common 15%, uncommon 20%, rare 25%, epic 30%, legendary 40%. A crop with an extra Harvest Bounty roll can independently find a Vine at 5%. The second and third Greenhouses require the previously recorded 100/150 Vines plus 10/20 Compost Bundles after all first-plot slots are unlocked; the item page says those added Greenhouses come with all planting slots unlocked. No public slot layout, owned vine quantity or current upgrade tier has been confirmed, so these are acquisition routes rather than a profile-specific purchase or remaining cost. This pass has no Luna or live key call. Further Farming work should join these rules with the rest of the domain and validate in one batch; freeze still requires that broader review and resolution of any route-level overclaims.
